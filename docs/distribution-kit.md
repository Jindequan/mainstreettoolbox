## 2. Reddit — r/SideProject（主战场，可发独立帖）

**规则要点**：这个 sub 的用途就是分享自建项目，允许链接；要求参与讨论、勿刷屏重发；别用纯营销腔。

**标题**：
```
I built 25 free calculators for people who run restaurants, cleaning businesses, lawn crews and salons — no signup, everything runs in the browser
```

**正文**：
```
Small business owners do brutal pricing math constantly — "what should a deep clean of a 3/2 cost?", "is 32% food cost on this dish bad?", "does booth rent beat a 45% commission split?". Spreadsheets exist, but nobody keeps a spreadsheet at the walk-in cooler.

So I built Main Street Toolbox: 25 calculators organized by trade, plus a menu maker that runs actual menu engineering (Kasavana–Smith quadrants — stars, plowhorses, puzzles, dogs) and exports print-ready PDF/PNG menus.

Design rules I set for myself:
- No signup, ever. Type numbers, get an answer.
- Everything computes in the browser; nothing is stored server-side.
- No ads or affiliate links (for now at least — site is free).

The benchmarks are the interesting part: every calculator tells you not just the number but whether it's in a healthy band for the trade (food cost 28–35%, labor 25–35% of revenue, booth rent 15–25% of sales...).

Would genuinely like feedback: which trade needs the next batch of tools? Right now it's restaurants, cleaning, lawn care, construction, salon/barber, retail — 3-5 tools each.
```
