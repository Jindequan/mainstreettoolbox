## 2. Reddit — 回复模板（对准"正在提问的人"，重点是 r/sweatystartup）

**A. 清洁报价类**（搜 "how much should I charge" / "pricing" — 这类帖几乎每周出现）
> The number that matters is your hourly floor, not the house. A standard 3bd/2ba usually lands $110–140 for 2.5–4 hours of work — deep cleans run 40–50% above that. I got tired of doing this math for people so I built a free calculator that takes beds/baths/type/frequency and gives you a range plus the hours: [cleaning estimate calculator]. No signup, nothing to install. Check the number it gives you against what people say here and price accordingly.

**B. 小费分账类**（r/KitchenConfidential, r/Serverlife — 搜 "tip out"）
> If it helps: 70/20/10 between servers/support/bar is the common full-service split, and the one rule is deciding it before the shift and writing it down. I made a free tip-out calculator that shows each role's pool and per-person amount [link] — screenshot it for the close-out. (Also worth knowing: managers can't take from a mandatory pool under the FLSA.)

**C. 美业 booth rent**（r/Barber, r/Hairstylist — 搜 "booth rent vs commission"）
> Quick math worth doing before you decide: commission nets you sales × split; booth nets sales − rent. Rent is sane at 15–25% of revenue. I built a free calculator for exactly this comparison [link] — put in your weekly sales, their split, and the rent quote and it tells you which way wins at your numbers.
