## 帖 2（日常回复模板：看到 "what should I charge" 类帖时用，可不带链接）

> The number that matters is your hourly floor, not the house. Figure out what you need to NET per hour (target take-home + ~25-30% for taxes + insurance + supplies ÷ realistic billable hours) — for most solo cleaners that floor lands around $45-60/hr billed. A standard 3bd/2ba is usually 2.5-4 hours of work, deep cleans run 40-50% above standard. Price the job off the floor, never quote the hourly to the client.
