  - region "Surcharge ban — common questions":
    - heading "Surcharge ban — common questions" [level=2]
    - term: How much will the surcharge ban cost my business?
    - definition: From 1 October 2026 you can no longer add a surcharge to card payments, so the card fee you used to pass on to customers becomes a cost your business absorbs. Compayr's surcharge ban cost calculator estimates that absorbed cost from your monthly card turnover and the surcharge percentage you currently add — shown per month and per year — and estimates the potential saving on a lower-cost plan. Figures are indicative estimates in Australian dollars; confirm current pricing with your provider.
    - term: When does the surcharge ban start and which payments are included?
    - definition: It starts on 1 October 2026 and applies to consumer card surcharging on eftpos, Mastercard and Visa. American Express has confirmed it follows the same rule. The ban is enforced through the card schemes' operating rules rather than by legislation.
    - term: Does Compayr both explain the surcharge ban and calculate what it will cost me?
    - definition: Yes. Compayr's October 2026 Reform Hub and Knowledge Centre explain what changes and which payments are covered, the surcharge calculator estimates the cost of the ban to your specific business (the surcharge you currently recover and the card cost you will absorb from 1 October 2026), and the comparison tool then ranks 18 providers (22 listings) by estimated card acceptance cost at your real turnover and card mix — so you can carry that cost for less.
    - term: Is the surcharge ban calculator free, and is it financial advice?
    - definition: The calculator and the full provider comparison are free and self-service, with no account required. Figures are indicative estimates only and are not financial advice — always confirm current pricing with the provider.
