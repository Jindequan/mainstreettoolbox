How to work this list

The full detail is the anchor

It's the package everything is compared against. Maintain services (wash, wax) fill the calendar between full details.

Pet hair is the built-in upsell

$50 flat, 30 minutes — and nearly every interior job has some. Ask before you start; add it as a line.

Maintenance washes on a route

Every 2 weeks on a route = predictable income at a discount that costs you less than acquisition.

Metro pricing

Major metros run 30–60% above the national band — the $389 full detail in LA is the same job as $225 in Tulsa.

Add-ons are lines, not bundles

Screens, pet hair, engine bay — show each as its own line. Silent bundling reads as padding.

How to judge YOUR local market — the five-step method

  1. Start from the national band — pages 1–2 of this kit.
  2. Apply the regional adjustment — coastal metros +20–50% across our research; small towns slightly under. Your state's cost-of-living index is a reasonable proxy.
  3. Compute your cost floor — (your loaded hours + materials/supplies) for the job. No quote below the floor, ever. It's the line between a business and a hobby.
  4. Validate locally — three published local prices (Angi/HomeGuide zip-level or competitor pages) tell you where the market actually clears.
  5. Set your band and review quarterly — booked out two weeks straight? Move up. Empty for a month? Your floor is above your market.

Worked example — Full detail (in+out), mid-size SUV, Denver CO

Step 1. National band: $150–300, typical $225.

Step 2. Regional: Denver is a solid mid-cost metro, mild premium (+10–20%) → $250–290.

Step 3. Cost floor: 4 hrs solo × $35 loaded + $12 supplies = $152. $275 yields ~45% margin.

Step 4. Validate: 3 local shops' posted prices; position $10 above the median with a better guarantee.

Same job, three cities, three prices — the metro you stand in is a pricing input, not an excuse.

Strategy — the five plays that move the number

The full detail anchors the menu

It's the package every other price is judged against. Keep it rich and central; maintain services fill the calendar around it.

Add-ons are separate lines

Pet hair $50, engine bay $80, headlights $130 — every add-on is its own line item. Silent bundling reads as padding and hides your best margin.

The route is the second business

Biweekly maintenance washes at a discount are annuity income — the discount costs less than acquiring a new full detail.

Sell the guarantee, not the price

'Come back within 48 hours if it rains' or a 7-day satisfaction re-clean beats a $20 discount and costs almost nothing.
