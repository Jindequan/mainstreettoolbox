How to work this list

The full detail is the anchor

It's the package everything is compared against. Maintain services (wash, wax) fill the calendar between full details.

Pet hair is the built-in upsell

$50 flat, 30 minutes — and nearly every interior job has some. Ask before you start; add it as a line.

Maintenance washes on a route

Every 2 weeks on a route = predictable income at a discount that costs you less than acquisition.

Metro pricing

Major metros run 30–60% above the national band — the $389 full detail in LA is the same job as $225 in Tulsa.

Add-ons are lines, not bundles

Screens, pet hair, engine bay — show each as its own line. Silent bundling reads as padding.
