How to use this database

  • Every row is a band, not a price. Your market, your cost structure and the job in front of you decide where inside the band you land.
  • Confidence grades: ★★★ = cross-checked across two or more independent named sources · ★★ = named industry sources or synthesis across our cited guides · ★ = working guideline (use to sanity-check, not to quote) · ◆ = arithmetic rule (definition, not market data).
  • Where a publisher's own range differs from our band, that is expected: our band is the synthesis across sources. Every source is named so you can check the raw numbers.
  • US national ranges unless noted. Coastal metros typically run 20–50% above; small-town markets slightly under.

Method

Bands are compiled and cross-checked from public industry cost guides (Angi, HomeGuide, LawnStarter, Thumbtack, HomeAdvisor, Jobber and others), trade-operator communities (LawnSite, Reddit operator forums), and Main Street Toolbox's own cited guides (cleaning prices, post-construction rates, food cost, booth rent vs commission). Ranges were last verified September 2026. Nothing in this document is a manufactured number: if we could not source it, it is not here.
