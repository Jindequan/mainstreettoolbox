What this adds beyond the free page

The free page at mainstreettoolbox.com/benchmarks/ shows our synthesis bands. This database adds the detail behind them: six extra rows of snow-removal and painting depth not on the page, a "raw ranges as published" column showing exactly what each source publishes (so you can see the spread, not just our synthesis), a filterable Excel workbook, and free quarterly refreshes for buyers.

How to use

  • Every row is a band, not a price — your market, costs and the job decide where inside it you land.
  • Confidence grades: ★★★ = cross-checked across 2+ independent named sources · ★★ = named sources or cited-guide synthesis · ★ = working guideline · ◆ = arithmetic rule.
  • The raw column shows each publisher's own range where we recorded it — a skeptical reader can see the spread, not just our conclusion.
  • US national ranges unless noted. Coastal metros run 20–50% above; small towns slightly under.

Method

Compiled and cross-checked from public industry cost guides (Angi, HomeGuide, LawnStarter, Thumbtack, HomeAdvisor, Jobber and others), trade-operator communities (LawnSite, operator forums), and Main Street Toolbox's own cited guides. Last verified September 2026. Nothing here is a manufactured number: if we could not source it, it is not here.
