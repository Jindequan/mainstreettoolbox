How to work this list

Bundles beat hourly

An hourly quote punishes you for being fast. A bundle folds your drive time, trip cost and cleanup into one honest number — clients compare bundles, not clocks.

The bundle math

(hours × your target rate) + materials markup + trip fee, per job. If a bundle loses money on paper, split it into two jobs or drop it.

Minimum visit charge

Never take a job below your smallest bundle + trip fee. 'Can you just look at it' calls get the free 10-minute walkthrough and a written quote — not a price shouted over the phone.

Materials markup

Bill materials at cost + 10–15%, stated on the quote. The surprise on the invoice is what kills repeat work.

When to raise

Booked two weeks out for a month straight? Raise your top two bundles ~10%. Demand is telling you the number was low.
