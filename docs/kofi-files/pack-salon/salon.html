How to work this list

Price your chair, not the market

Your real cost per bookable hour = (booth rent or commission target) ÷ booked hours. Services below that number are charity, and charity doesn't buy product.

Color is the margin engine

Highlights take 2–3× a cut's time — price them honestly at 2–3×. A $200 full highlight is a $70 cut with more chair time, not a luxury.

Raise with a new menu

Announce two weeks ahead, raise the underpriced services most, keep the increase under 10%/year for regulars.

Commission negotiation

New stylists keep 40–50%, moving toward 60% with a full book — that's the standard ladder. Know your number before the lease conversation.

Retail adds quiet revenue

50–100% margin on retail products adds 5–10% to revenue with zero chair time.
