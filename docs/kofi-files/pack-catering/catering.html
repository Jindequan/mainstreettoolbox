How to work this list

Drop-off is a volume game

No staff, no rentals, thin margins, fast turns — it fills calendars between full-service bookings. Don't price it like staffed service.

Buffet math

Target 30% food cost: price = ingredient cost × 3.3. If the menu costs more than that, cut SKUs before you cut margin.

Staffing prices separately

Servers add $25–40 per person in real cost. Never bake it in — itemize it, or every quote looks high.

The bar is the profit center

Beverage service margins run 60–70%. Lead with it on every quote.

Contract anchors

50% deposit at booking, final headcount 7 days out, balance due day-of. Below ~50 guests, sell drop-off or refer out.
